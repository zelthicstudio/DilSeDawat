Privacy Policy for DilSeDawat

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to DilSeDawat, an offline-first meal planning, ingredient estimation, and guest management mobile application developed by Zelthic ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed through your use of DilSeDawat. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is stored, how third-party services operate within the app, and your legal privacy rights.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy, please contact us at zelthic.studio@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

DilSeDawat is designed to function primarily offline. We do not maintain user accounts, databases, or cloud servers that store your personal identifiers.

Data Collected via Integrated SDKs:

  • Device Advertising Identifier: Collected automatically by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to request and display non-personalized or personalized advertisements.
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: Data such as operating system version, device model, IP address, and crash reports automatically collected by Google Play Services and AdMob for fraud prevention, network operations, and ad analytics.
  • Anonymous Purchase & Entitlement Data: Managed via RevenueCat when in-app purchases are enabled (currently disabled for launch). This includes anonymous transaction identifiers generated by Google Play Store billing without exposing personal identity or credit card numbers.

Data We DO NOT Collect:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do NOT collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or account login credentials.
  • No Device Sensors or Media: We do NOT request or access your device location (GPS), camera, photos, microphone, or contacts.
  • No Cloud Data Syncing: None of your entered meal plans, guest lists, ingredient prices, or custom recipes are uploaded to our servers.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

All core app data generated during your use of DilSeDawat—including dawat (event) plans, guest names, ingredient price modifications, and grocery checklists—is stored strictly on your local device using a sandboxed SQLite database via the expo-sqlite framework. Sensitive app preferences may be secured using the operating system's secure storage (expo-secure-store).

This data never leaves your device. It is fully contained within the app's secure sandboxed storage environment provided by your device's operating system.

App usage events (such as screen navigation and feature taps) may be logged locally in development mode only. These logs are never transmitted to us or any third party and are cleared when you uninstall the app.

4. How We Use Information

The limited data processed by the app is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Displaying Advertisements: Delivering banner and interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob to support ongoing development.
  • App Maintenance & Stability: Diagnostics and performance telemetry provided by Google Play Services to fix crashes and optimize user experience.
  • Processing Purchases: Validating optional in-app purchase receipts (e.g., removing ads) when in-app billing is active.

5. Third-Party Services

DilSeDawat integrates trusted third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) to provide core functionality and ad support. Each third-party provider operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
    • Purpose: Serves banner and interstitial ads within the app.
    • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (In-App Purchase Management)
    • Purpose: Manages user purchase receipts and entitlements.
    • Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services (Platform Services)
    • Purpose: Provides Android system integrations, security checks, and Play Store installation management.
    • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your data.

The only data shared externally is the device advertising ID and standard SDK diagnostics transmitted directly to Google AdMob to fetch ad content. No personal information, guest data, or meal planning records are ever shared with third parties.

7. Data Security

We take data protection seriously:

  • Network Encryption: All communications between integrated SDKs (such as AdMob) and remote endpoints use secure Transport Layer Security (HTTPS encryption).
  • Local Application Sandbox: Local application data is isolated inside your operating system's protected app sandbox, preventing unauthorized access by other mobile applications installed on your device.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all your planning data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data remains stored on your device as long as DilSeDawat remains installed.
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete all stored app data at any time by:
    1. Uninstalling the DilSeDawat application from your device, OR
    2. Clearing app data and cache via your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > DilSeDawat > Storage > Clear Data).

9. Children's Privacy

DilSeDawat is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at zelthic.studio@gmail.com so we can take appropriate measures.

10. GDPR Rights (EU Users)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, you are entitled to specific rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to Access & Data Portability: All your data remains on your device and is accessible directly through the app UI.
  • Right to Erasure & Rectification: You can edit or delete any stored data directly inside DilSeDawat or delete all data by uninstalling the application.
  • Right to Object / Restrict Processing: You can restrict ad tracking by resetting or disabling your device's Advertising ID in your device system settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to file a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (DPA) in the EU member state where you reside.

For any GDPR-related inquiries or formal requests, email us at zelthic.studio@gmail.com.

11. CCPA Rights (California Users)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know & Delete: You have the right to request information regarding data collection and to request deletion. (As noted, all user-entered data resides solely on your device).
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Information: We do NOT sell your personal information or user data to third parties.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app practices, legal requirements, or SDK integrations. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of DilSeDawat after policy modifications constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
